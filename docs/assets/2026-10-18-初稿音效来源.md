# Original procedural audio

These seven WAV files were created for this demo with deterministic mathematical synthesis in `tools/make_audio.py`. They contain no downloaded recordings, source samples, commercial music, voice, or external audio assets.

All files use uncompressed 16-bit PCM at 22,050 Hz. `ambient.wav` is a 12-second stereo loop; cues are mono. Total WAV size: 1,308,312 bytes.

| File | Length | Sound |
|---|---:|---|
| ambient.wav | 12 s | Filtered night wind and a soft low harmonic drone, seamless periodic synthesis |
| pickup.wav | 0.65 s | Two restrained glass chimes |
| rescue.wav | 1.15 s | Three gentle rising notes |
| extract.wav | 1.65 s | Four rising notes marking safe return |
| down.wav | 0.95 s | Rounded descending tone and soft air texture |
| step.wav | 0.16 s | Low soft footfall with filtered dust texture |
| danger.wav | 1.10 s | Two low ominous pulses |

Validation: all seven WAVs re-open with Python's standard `wave` reader; rate, channels, 16-bit depth, frame lengths, noncompressed format and total size were checked. Peak amplitude is below 0.39 for every cue and below 0.17 for ambience, with no clipping. The ambient components and modulation repeat over exactly 12 seconds; filtered noise is synthesized periodically in frequency space.

`game/sound.gd` supplies a six-voice cue pool, seamless ambient loop, mute/unmute and footsteps with a 330 ms cooldown. Ambient volume defaults to -27 dB, event cues -14 dB, footsteps -22 dB. Copy audio assets under the Godot project's `assets/audio/` and the helper under `game/`.

These are intentionally restrained environmental sounds, not a full musical soundtrack.
